### Post Copy (Ready to Publish on LinkedIn)

Excited to share my Master’s Capstone project at the University of Europe for Applied Sciences (Potsdam Campus): **VeriCBAM — A Data-Driven Decision Support System for Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) Consistency Screening**. 🇪🇺🌍

Starting in 2026, European industrial importers must verify self-reported greenhouse gas emissions for goods like **Cement** and **Steel** imported into the EU under Regulation (EU) 2023/956. However, commissioning physical on-site audits for thousands of overseas industrial installations poses severe logistical bottlenecks.

To address this challenge, **VeriCBAM** acts as an explainable screening copilot that cross-checks self-reported emissions declarations against independent multi-source evidence before costly inspections are commissioned:

🔹 **First-Principles Chemical Stoichiometry:** Enforcing thermodynamic mass-balance lower bounds for cement clinker calcination and steelmaking reduction routes.  
🔹 **Multi-Sensor Satellite Earth Observation:** Integrating 30m USGS Landsat-8/9 thermal infrared (TIRS) core heat signatures, daily 375m NASA VIIRS active operating calendars, and high-resolution optical rail-yard freight activity to verify operational presence from orbit.  
🔹 **Longitudinal Trajectories & Co-Pollutant Fingerprinting:** Evaluating leave-one-year-out historical baselines and NOx/CO2 combustion ratios from public environmental compliance registries (EEA E-PRTR).  
🔹 **Transparent Evidence Fusion:** Synthesizing all streams through a confidence-gated Bayesian log-odds engine (achieving <3.5% false alarms on compliant facilities) and a supervised triage queue delivering a 2.71x audit prioritization lift.  

The platform includes an interactive, browser-based decision cockpit with a 1-click **Official Pre-Audit Screening Dossier (PDF)** generator for customs declarants, accredited verification bodies, and industrial importers.
