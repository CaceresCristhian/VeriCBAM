## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, mission-critical regulatory audit interface engineered for cross-border carbon tariff enforcement and industrial emissions telemetry.

### Tone & Personality
- **Sovereign & Authoritative:** Evokes institutional integrity, legal enforceability, and ironclad compliance aligned with European Commission regulatory protocols.
- **Forensic & Analytical:** Prioritizes high information density, rapid anomaly detection, stoichiometric scrutiny, and statistical confidence levels over decorative filler.
- **Instrumental & Tactical:** Interfaces operate like avionics dashboards or industrial supervisory control (SCADA) telemetry, providing real-time data transparency, provenance tracking, and risk triage.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Technical Minimalist / Precision Telemetry**:
- Grounded in ultra-deep obsidian and slate substrates with crisp geometric delineation.
- Zero ornamental glow or skeuomorphic distraction; depth is achieved via surface luminance stepping and strict border enclosures.
- Highly functional data density with micro-status indicators, tabular telemetry grids, and disciplined cryptographic audit cues.

## Colors

The color system delivers high legibility and unequivocal status demarcation across high-density data tables and verification workflows in an ambient dark environment.

### Core Foundation
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Deep obsidian ground minimizing eye strain during prolonged forensic review.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#111827`):** Primary panel, card, and telemetry block backing.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1E293B`):** Elevated data containers, hover states, and structural row backgrounds.
- **Border Subtle (`#1E293B`):** Structural partition line for secondary divisions.
- **Border Defined (`#334155`):** Explicit component bounding and active container edges.

### Accent & Telemetry Indicators
- **Cyan Accent (`#38BDF8` / `#0EA5E9`):** Primary interaction anchors, key verification parameters, system breadcrumbs, and selected active telemetry nodes.
- **Compliant / Consistent (`#10B981` / `#059669`):** Direct emissions data verified against regulatory CBAM benchmark defaults without discrepancy.
- **Marginal / Under Scrutiny (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Statistical variance detected within borderline confidence intervals (±2σ), requiring documentation cross-reference.
- **Critical Violation Risk (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`):** Gross under-reporting, missing precursor data, unaccredited laboratory signatures, or tariff evasion risk flags.
- **Indeterminate / Evidence Deficit (`#6366F1` / `#64748B`):** Missing installation boundary declarations, unverified energy grid factors, or pending third-party verifier attestation.

### Text Hierarchy
- **Text High-Contrast (`#F8FAFC`):** Quantitative metrics, metric labels, primary headers.
- **Text Medium-Contrast (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary metadata, unit denominators (e.g., `tCO2e/t`), stoichiometric formulas.
- **Text Low-Contrast (`#64748B`):** Inactive controls, regulatory footnotes, disabled table columns.

## Typography

Typography establishes an unambiguous dichotomy between qualitative audit directives and quantitative stoichiometric telemetry.

### Font Roles
- **Inter (Headlines & Interface Body):** Handles all natural-language narrative, regulatory directive titles, auditor annotations, and core navigational text. Uses neutral, systematic letterforms for neutral scannability.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Equations & Verification Data):** Mandated for all numerical data, CN codes (Combined Nomenclature), emission intensities ($tCO_2e/t$), sigma discrepancies ($\sigma$), installation identifiers, and cryptographic hash strings. Tabular figures prevent layout shifting during real-time data streaming.

### Usage Standards
- Numbers inside tables and comparative audit grids must always render in `JetBrains Mono` to ensure monospaced vertical alignment across decimal points.
- Capitalization for telemetry labels and status states must remain uppercase with positive tracking (+0.05em) for high-contrast legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by high-density operational grid lines, utilizing a 12-column responsive fluid grid with precise horizontal rhythm.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with fixed side navigation (280px), `gutter-lg` (1.5rem / 24px), and `margin-lg` (2rem / 32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with collapsible sidebar icon dock (64px), `gutter` (1rem / 16px), and `margin` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, full-width fluid stacked cards, horizontal scroll for raw emission matrices, `gutter` (0.75rem / 12px), and `margin` (1rem / 16px).

### Spacing Principles
- Compact vertical pacing: Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for related micro-telemetry (e.g., metric label to unit value).
- Structural breathing room: Use `space-md` for internal card padding and `space-lg` to isolate distinct regulatory sections (e.g., direct emissions vs. precursor chain verification).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft dropshadows, diffuse colored glows, or decorative blurring. Depth is conveyed strictly through structural containment, surface lightness progression, and hairline framing.

### Surface Tiers
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#0B0F17` – System viewport backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules):** `#111827` with 1px border `#1E293B` – Audit log panels, baseline tables, parameter sidebars.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspections):** `#1E293B` with 1px border `#334155` – Hovered table rows, sub-meters, expanded formula cards.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Inspectors):** `#111827` with 1px border `#38BDF8` – Flyout audit inspectors, cryptographic proof dialogs, and critical warning sheets.

### Edge Illumination & Focus
Active elements achieve prominence through a single-pixel accent highlight (`#38BDF8` or status-specific tokens) rather than ambient shadow spread. This maintains a sharp, tactical display appearance akin to professional laboratory or compliance hardware.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a strictly architectural, sharp aesthetic (`roundedness: 0`, 0px border radius) across all operational surfaces.

### Principles
- Zero rounding conveys regulatory rigidity, mathematical precision, and technical finality.
- Cards, chips, data cells, input fields, and modal containers use precise 90-degree corners.
- Visual rhythm relies on geometric cuts, segmented borders, and nested hairline frames instead of corner smoothing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Telemetry Action:** Solid background `#38BDF8`, high-contrast obsidian text `#0B0F17`, font weight 600. Border: 1px solid `#38BDF8`. Hover state transitions to `#0EA5E9`.
- **Secondary Auditor Action:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`. Hover state: background `#1E293B`, border `#64748B`.
- **Destructive / Flag Action:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#EF4444`, text `#EF4444`. Hover state: background `#EF4444`, text `#0B0F17`.
- **Shape & Spacing:** 0px radius; vertical padding `0.5rem`, horizontal padding `1rem`; typography `JetBrains Mono` 12px uppercase.

### Verification Chips & Risk Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Encased in a 1px border matching the classification token, paired with an ultra-subtle tinted surface (opacity 10%). Text is strictly uppercase `JetBrains Mono` 11px.
  - *Consistent:* `#10B981` border and text.
  - *Potential Inconsistency:* `#F59E0B` border and text.
  - *High Inconsistency Risk:* `#EF4444` border and text.
  - *Insufficient Evidence:* `#6366F1` border and text.
- Micro-pip indicator: A 4x4px solid geometric square preceding the label text.

### Data Tables & Stoichiometric Grids
- **Header Cells:** Background `#0B0F17`, text `#64748B`, font `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase, border-bottom 1px solid `#334155`.
- **Data Rows:** Background alternates between transparent and `#111827`. Hover state highlights with `#1E293B` and an active 2px left border accent in `#38BDF8`.
- **Cell Content:** Left-aligned text for installation metadata; right-aligned tabular numbers for quantities, specific emissions, and benchmark deltas.

### Input Fields & Filter Controls
- **Base Style:** Background `#0B0F17`, border 1px solid `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Font `JetBrains Mono` 13px.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid outline `#38BDF8`, no exterior glow or drop shadow.
- **Embedded Units:** Trailing fixed unit blocks (e.g., `EUR/t`, `MWh`, `tCO2e`) enclosed in a subtle `#1E293B` box with text `#94A3B8`.

### Telemetry Cards
- Containers configured with `#111827` surface, defined by a 1px `#1E293B` border.
- Top-right corner displays the analytical confidence level (e.g., `CONF: 99.4%` or `σ: +2.8`).
- Primary KPI displayed in `telemetry-metric` typography, accompanied by stoichiometric sub-formulas or precursor breakdown bars directly beneath.

### Specialized Industry Components
- **Stoichiometric Delta Meter:** A segmented horizontal bar depicting baseline EU benchmark emissions versus reported production installation emissions, shaded dynamically with green, amber, or crimson segments.
- **Chain-of-Custody Breadcrumb:** Stepped rectangular breadcrumb nodes showing origin country, smelter/facility ID, declared electricity factor, and verifying body accreditation hash.
